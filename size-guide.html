How to Measure Your Feet

Step-by-Step Measuring Guide
  1. Prepare: Stand on a piece of paper with your heel against a wall
  2. Trace: Have someone trace around your foot, keeping the pencil perpendicular
  3. Measure: Measure from the longest toe to the back of the heel
  4. Repeat: Measure both feet and use the larger measurement
  5. Add space: Add 0.5-1cm (0.2-0.4 inches) for comfort
Pro Tip: Measure your feet at the end of the day when they're at their largest, and always measure while standing.

Brand-Specific Sizing Notes

Nike
  • Generally true to size
  • Air Max runs slightly large
  • Consider half size down for snug fit
Adidas
  • True to size for most models
  • Ultraboost runs slightly small
  • Consider half size up for comfort
Jordan
  • Generally true to size
  • Retro models may run narrow
  • Consider wide width if available
Converse
  • Runs large - size down
  • Consider half to full size down
  • Chuck Taylor sizing is consistent

Tips for Perfect Fit

Time of Day

Measure your feet in the afternoon when they're at their largest size.

Wear Socks

Measure while wearing the type of socks you'll wear with the shoes.

Weight Distribution

Stand with your full weight on both feet when measuring.
